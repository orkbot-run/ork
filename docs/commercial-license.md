# Ork commercial license - draft for legal review

This is an unsigned agreement template. It grants no rights until the parties
complete the order form and sign the agreement. Have qualified counsel approve
it before offering it to a customer. Pricing is by individual quotation.

## License grant

Subject to the signed order form and payment of the agreed fee, the licensor
grants the customer a nonexclusive license during the agreed term to use,
reproduce, and modify the covered Ork work for the permitted use. Customer
hosting, distribution, sublicensing, and use by affiliates are permitted only
to the extent the order form expressly includes them. The order form must
identify any patent rights included in the grant.

This grant covers only rights that the licensor owns or is authorized to
license. Rakazo material remains under Apache 2.0. Third-party software and
assets remain under their own terms. The customer retains those independent
rights, and must preserve their required notices. Nothing in this agreement
withdraws rights under a license previously received.

## Ownership and customer changes

Each party retains ownership of its existing material. The customer owns its
original changes, subject to rights in the underlying software. There is no
trademark license, exclusivity, or obligation to contribute changes back unless
separately agreed. Redistribution must include the applicable license and
attribution materials and stay within the order form's express scope.

## Delivery and support

The order form identifies the delivered version. Later releases, maintenance,
hosting, support, security response times, and uptime commitments are included
only when a signed schedule says so. The customer remains responsible for its
infrastructure and separately contracted model or integration services.

## Fees and term

The fee, currency, tax treatment, payment date, term, and renewal rules must be
completed in the order form. Additional scope needs a written amendment. An
inquiry, source download, or invoice request alone does not grant a license.

## Warranty and liability

Except for express commitments in a signed schedule and rights that cannot be
excluded by law, the software is supplied as is. Counsel must finalize the
warranties, liability cap, exclusions, and any indemnities for the relevant
jurisdiction. This template supplies no indemnity by default.

## Breach and termination

A party may terminate for a material breach that remains uncorrected 30 days
after written notice, subject to mandatory law and the signed order form.
On expiry or termination the commercial grant ends. Any continued use of the
covered work must have another valid license. Rights independently held under
Apache or third-party licenses remain intact. The parties must agree how to
handle backups, customer continuity, accrued payments, and surviving clauses.

## Entire agreement

The signed order form, these finalized terms, and expressly incorporated
schedules form the agreement. Changes require both parties' written agreement.
Counsel must resolve precedence, assignment, notices, applicable law, and dispute
resolution before signature. Do not publish a completed customer order form in
this repository.
