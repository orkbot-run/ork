# Ork commercial license - draft for legal review

This unsigned template is for legal review. It grants no rights until both
parties sign a completed order form incorporating the finalized terms.
Qualified counsel must approve the agreement before it is offered to a customer.
The order form sets the monthly seat price.

## License grant

While the subscription is paid and active, the licensor grants the customer a
nonexclusive license to use, reproduce, and modify the covered Ork work on the
authorized deployments for the uses stated in the signed order form.
The grant covers the web application, desktop application, and mobile
application identified there. Only named affiliates are covered.
The order form identifies any included patent rights.

This grant covers only rights the licensor owns or is authorized to license.
Inherited Apache-licensed material remains under Apache 2.0, and third-party
software and assets retain their own licenses. Rights independently available
under Apache 2.0, PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, or another applicable license
remain available on those licenses' terms. The limits of this commercial grant
do not restrict those independent rights or withdraw any earlier license grant.
Preserve required license texts and attribution notices.

## Seats

One seat covers one person. Each person accessing or using the covered Ork work
under this commercial grant needs an assigned, paid seat, including a person
whose access is through a shared or service account.

A person's seat covers that person's use across the authorized deployments and
the web, desktop, and mobile applications included in the same order form.
Using several devices or authorized deployments does not require extra seats
for that person. AI bots and automated agents require no seats.
Their use does not replace the seat required for a person using Ork through them.

The customer may reassign a seat only after removing the previous person's
access from every deployment covered by the order form. Concurrent seat
sharing is prohibited.
Assignments must stay within the paid seat count.

## Customer hosting and separate grants

Hosting Ork for third parties is covered only when the signed order form
expressly authorizes it. Every person using that hosted Ork installation under
the commercial grant must have a paid seat covered by the order form.
Running bots for those people does not remove the seat requirement.

Embedding Ork in another product, reselling or sublicensing it, and OEM
distribution require a separate signed agreement. The standard grant also
excludes using the covered work to develop or offer a product whose principal
functionality competes with Ork. Any such use needs its own signed terms.
These exclusions define the scope of the commercial grant and leave independent
Apache, PolyForm, and third-party license rights unchanged.

## Ownership and customer changes

Each party retains ownership of its existing material, and the customer owns
its original changes subject to rights in the underlying software.
Distribution of modified Ork work under this commercial grant is allowed only
within the scope expressly stated in the signed order form or a separate signed
agreement. That distribution must include the applicable licenses and notices.
The grant supplies no trademark rights or exclusivity. Contributions back to Ork
are voluntary unless separately agreed.

## Delivery and support

Specify covered versions and delivery in the order form.
An entitlement to later releases, maintenance, or support requires an express
commitment in the signed order form or a signed schedule.

The software subscription does not include an Ork-hosted service.
Hosting, service availability, security response times, and any service-level
agreement require separate signed terms. The customer remains responsible for
its infrastructure and separately contracted model or integration services.

## Fees and term

Seat fees are charged monthly in advance. Renewal is monthly until cancelled.
Use the billing date in the order form, which must also state the monthly price
per seat and payment details before signature.

Added seats are charged pro rata for the remainder of the current monthly
billing period and at the full monthly rate from the next renewal.
Seat reductions take effect at the end of the current paid period, with the
reduced count applying from the next renewal.

The customer may cancel by written notice at any time. Cancellation takes
effect at the end of the current paid monthly period, with access under the
commercial grant continuing until then. No further monthly renewal follows.

The licensor must give at least 30 days' written notice of a seat price change.
The changed price applies at a monthly renewal after that notice period ends.
The customer may cancel before that renewal. Other changes to the agreed scope
require a written amendment signed by both parties.

An inquiry, source download, or invoice request alone does not grant a license.

## Warranty and liability

Except for express commitments in a signed schedule and rights that cannot be
excluded by law, the software is supplied as is. Counsel must finalize the
warranties, liability cap, exclusions, and any indemnities for the relevant
jurisdiction. This template supplies no indemnity by default.

## Breach and termination

A party may terminate for a material breach that remains uncorrected 30 days
after written notice, subject to mandatory law and the signed order form.
On expiry or termination the commercial grant ends. Continued use of the
covered work must have another valid license. Independent rights under Apache,
PolyForm, and third-party licenses remain intact. The parties must agree how to
handle backups, customer continuity, accrued payments, and surviving clauses.

## Entire agreement

The signed order form, these finalized terms, and expressly incorporated
schedules form the agreement. Changes require both parties' written agreement.
Counsel must resolve precedence, assignment, notices, applicable law, and dispute
resolution before signature. Do not publish a completed customer order form in
this repository.
